Recent publications, presentations, awards, student milestones, and updates from SocialNLP.

Aug 2026

Our paper on cancer misinformation is accepted to Findings of EMNLP

Our paper, Beyond Binary Detection: A Multi-Dimensional Taxonomy of Cancer Misinformation on Reddit, is accepted to Findings of EMNLP. The work develops a multidimensional framework for characterizing cancer misinformation online.

2026

New TACL paper on trust and distrust in generative AI

Our longitudinal study examines how trust and distrust toward GenAI are expressed and how they evolve over time.

2026

“Like a Therapist, But Not” published in Findings of ACL

Led by Elham Aghakhani, this work studies Reddit narratives of AI use in mental-health contexts.

Layla Bouzoubaa graduates from the PhD program

Layla is SocialNLP's first graduated PhD student and begins a postdoctoral position after graduation.

2026

Reddit After Roe appears at ICWSM

Aria Pessianzadeh and collaborators examine abortion narratives, information practices, and barriers after Dobbs.

Phenotypes of Stigma published in Social Science & Medicine

Our work identifies distinct forms of stigma expressed in lived-experience narratives shared by people who use drugs.

Problem-Solving Therapy work appears in Findings of ACL
